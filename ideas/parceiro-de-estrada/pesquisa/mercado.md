# Mercado (TAM/SAM/SOM) para "Parceiro de Estrada": copiloto de voz para caminhoneiros no Brasil

Notes compiled 2026-11-21. Source tiers: **[P]** = primary/official (ANTT, Senatran/IBGE, Novo Caged/RAIS); **[A]** = industry association or analyst press release (CNT, NTC, IPTC, Berg Insight); **[S]** = secondary/paid-report teaser or company marketing (weaker; flagged). Every number carries its reference year.

## Q1. Structure of the market: drivers, carriers (RNTRC), fleet, concentration

### Takeaway
Brazil has about 2.85M vehicles registered in the RNTRC (Dec/2025) and about 4.1M trucks plus truck-tractors on Senatran's plated fleet (Dec/2024). Roughly 0.7M truck drivers have CLT jobs and about 0.77M people are registered as autônomos (TAC). The market is very fragmented: 73% of carriers are TACs whose fleet averages 24 years old, and most ETCs are micro or small companies. Companies (ETC) hold 66% of the registered fleet, and that is where the buyers are.

### Cited Findings
**RNTRC / ANTT (primary):**
- Carriers with an active, pending or suspended RNTRC in Dec/2025: **TAC 769,253; ETC (incl. "ETC equiparado a TAC") 280,036; CTC 516; total 1,049,805**. Before the 2024 revalidação the series had dropped to a low of 740,614. [P] — [ANTT Anuário TRC 2025, Tabela 1](https://www.gov.br/antt/pt-br/assuntos/cargas/dadostrc/anuario_trc_2025_antt.pdf)
- Share of carriers at end-2025: TACs **73.27%** of carriers but only **32.56%** of RNTRC vehicles. ETCs **26.67%** of carriers and **66.07%** of vehicles. CTCs 0.04% of carriers and 1.55% of vehicles. [P] — [ANTT Anuário TRC 2025](https://www.gov.br/antt/pt-br/assuntos/cargas/dadostrc/anuario_trc_2025_antt.pdf)
- Vehicles tied to RNTRC carriers in Dec/2025: **TAC 926,988; ETC 1,880,590; CTC 38,624; total 2,846,202**, up 8.95% vs Dec/2024 (2,612,344). This count **includes implementos** (reboques and semirreboques), so it is not a count of tractive units. [P] — [ANTT Anuário TRC 2025, Tabela 2](https://www.gov.br/antt/pt-br/assuntos/cargas/dadostrc/anuario_trc_2025_antt.pdf)
- Average age of tractive vehicles (Dec/2025): **ETC 9.8 years; ETC equiparado 15.5; CTC 15.6; TAC 24.4; overall 16.8**. About 40% of ETC tractive vehicles are 5 years old or less. Almost no TAC fleet is under 10 years old. [P] — [ANTT Anuário TRC 2025, Tabela 3](https://www.gov.br/antt/pt-br/assuntos/cargas/dadostrc/anuario_trc_2025_antt.pdf)
- ETC size: the Receita Federal classified 238,701 ETCs (77% of the total), and most active ETCs are **Microempresa or EPP**. The exact percentages are in a chart (Gráfico 15) that I could not extract as text. [P] — [ANTT Anuário TRC 2025](https://www.gov.br/antt/pt-br/assuntos/cargas/dadostrc/anuario_trc_2025_antt.pdf)
- Rules: a TAC is a natural person with up to 3 vehicles and up to 3 TAC-auxiliares. An "ETC equiparado a TAC" is a company with up to 3 vehicles. [P] — [ANTT Anuário TRC 2025](https://www.gov.br/antt/pt-br/assuntos/cargas/dadostrc/anuario_trc_2025_antt.pdf)
- Older ANTT snapshot for comparison (31/Jul/2022): 887,271 TAC, 283,393 ETC, 544 CTC, 2,603,878 vehicles. This is pre-revalidação. [S, aggregator snippet] — reported in search results citing ANTT; see [ANTT Anuário TRC 2024](https://www.gov.br/antt/pt-br/assuntos/cargas/dadostrc/anuario_trc_2024.pdf)

**Fleet (Senatran):**
- National plated fleet in Dec/2024: **3,158,606 caminhões + 948,214 caminhões-trator** (≈4.1M). This includes own-account (carga própria) and light/medium trucks. [P] — [IBGE Anuário Estatístico, tabela s5t2102 (fonte Senatran)](https://anuario.ibge.gov.br/images/aeb/2024/s5/2_pdf/s5t2102.pdf)
- New truck registrations in 2025: **110,973, down 8.6% vs 2024**. [S] — [ABCdoABC](https://www.abcdoabc.com.br/o-transporte-aprendeu-a-jogar-na-defensiva/)
- Conflicting figure: Sindicam cites 2.24M trucks "em circulação" in 2024, a different definition. [S] — [Mundo Logística](https://mundologistica.com.br/artigos/a-um-passo-maioridade-18-anos-frota-brasileira)

**Drivers:**
- CLT truck drivers in RAIS: **701,285 (2023) → 713,229 (2024), +1.7%**. Median remuneration in 2024 was **R$3,120/month**. The median at companies with 1,000+ vehicles is R$4,246, vs R$2,969 at companies with up to 50 vehicles. The annual payroll is R$29.4B. Net hiring fell 46.5% (+26.9k from Jul/23 to Jun/24 vs +14.4k from Jul/24 to Jun/25). [P] — [ANTT/SUROC infográfico "Perfil e Remuneração dos motoristas de caminhão" (ago/2025, RAIS 2024)](https://www.gov.br/antt/pt-br/assuntos/cargas/dadostrc/infograficoperfilmotoristasrais2024.pdf)
- Truck-driver hires (Novo Caged) in 2025: **534,972** (–1.18% vs 541,409 in 2024). This counts admissions only, which points to very high turnover against a stock of about 0.7M. [P] — [ANTT Anuário TRC 2025](https://www.gov.br/antt/pt-br/assuntos/cargas/dadostrc/anuario_trc_2025_antt.pdf)
- IPTC (2025): **541,588** active formal truck-driver jobs (a different cut from the ANTT/RAIS 713k, probably restricted to TRC CNAEs). It also counts **433,192 active "MEI Caminhoneiro"** (+25% in 2025) and **more than 11M** RENACH licence holders in categories C, D and E. [A] — [Mundo Logística citing IPTC (published 24/09/2026)](https://mundologistica.com.br/noticias/mei-caminhoneiro-cresceu-em-2025-e-emprego-clt-avancou)
- CNT Perfil dos Caminhoneiros (old waves): the 2016 sample was 68.4% autônomos and 31.6% employed. Average income in 2019 was R$5,011 for autônomos and R$3,721 for employed drivers. In 2019, 34.1% reported debts and 47% of autônomos had financed their truck. [A, dated] — [SETCESP](https://setcesp.org.br/noticias/caminhoneiros-aumentam-renda-mensal-media-em-18-em-tres-anos/); [CNT 2016 PDF](https://repositorio.itl.org.br/jspui/bitstream/123456789/152/1/Pesquisa%20CNT%20Perfil%20dos%20Caminhoneiros%202016.pdf)
- 88% of TRC companies report difficulty hiring drivers or agregados (NTC 2025/26). Driver shortage is a retention argument for the product. [A] — [Agência iNFRA](https://agenciainfra.com/blog/ntclogistica-falta-de-motoristas-trava-setor-de-transporte-de-cargas/); [SETCESP](https://setcesp.org.br/noticias/economia/falta-de-motoristas-afeta-88-das-empresas-do-transporte-rodoviario-de-cargas/)

**Concentration:**
- CNT (via Estadão, undated, likely older): 111.7k companies own 1.1M vehicles, an **average of 9.7 vehicles per company**. ILOS (Maurício Lima) says most of the sector is small companies with **3–4 trucks**. [S, weak/dated] — [Folha Vitória](https://www.folhavitoria.com.br/economia/hipotese-de-locaute-esbarra-em-pulverizacao/)
- Repom: about 7 in 10 road freights are executed by transportadoras. [S] — [Mercado & Consumo](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/30/12/2022/logistica/trasnportadoras-sao-responsaveis-por-7040-da-execucao-de-frete-no-pais/?noamp=mobile)

### Inferences
- Rough average for an ETC: 1.88M vehicles / 280k ETCs ≈ 6.7 vehicles, including implementos. With roughly half of those tractive, that is about 3–4 tractive units per ETC. The CO-level buyer (a carrier with 50+ trucks and its own control center) is therefore a small number of firms holding a large share of the fleet. The ANTT infographic's own cut points (≤50 vs ≥1,000 vehicles) suggest ANTT has this distribution, but it is not published in text.
- Number of drivers who could use the product: about 0.7M CLT + about 0.77M TAC ≈ 1.4–1.5M. Agregados (TACs working exclusively for one carrier) are reachable through carriers.

### Gaps
- **No public, current count of carriers by fleet size (10+, 50+, 100+ trucks).** The data exist in RNTRC open data (dados.antt.gov.br, frota by CPF/CNPJ) but would need to be downloaded and aggregated. This is a key piece of work still to do.
- The split of RNTRC vehicles into tractive units and implementos is not in the Anuário text.
- There is no current (2024–2026) CNT survey splitting autônomos and employed drivers.

## Q2. Fleet telematics, rastreamento, gerenciamento de risco, video telematics and DMS in Brazil

### Takeaway
Telematics in Brazil is large and mature, driven by insurers and risk managers who require tracking. Analyst estimates put revenue at about **USD 1.0–1.2B per year (2024–2026), growing 11–16% a year**, but the estimates vary in scope and are low quality. Berg Insight counts **about 10.2M active fleet-management units in Latin America (2025)**, but publishes no Brazil figure for free. Video telematics with fatigue detection is the fast-growing upsell, and vendors do not publish prices.

### Cited Findings
- Berg Insight, Fleet Management in Latin America (2nd ed., 2026): **10.2M active FM units in LatAm in 2025 → 20.6M in 2030 (15.2% CAGR)**. Gurtam, Michelin Connected Fleet and Geotab are the largest providers, each with "several hundred thousand" active units in the region. The 1st edition had 8.3M (2024) → 16.6M (2029). [A] — [GII listing of Berg 2nd ed.](https://www.giiresearch.com/report/ber2074816-fleet-management-latin-america-2nd-edition.html); [MarketResearch.com 1st ed.](https://www.marketresearch.com/Berg-Insight-v2702/Fleet-Management-Latin-America-Edition-41622994)
- Geotab: more than 300k active subscriptions in Latin America (2025) and more than 5M worldwide (Sep/2025). [S, company] — [Geotab press](https://www.geotab.com/pt-br/sala-de-imprensa/geotab-5m-veículos-conectados-global/)
- Gurtam/Wialon: Brazil is its 3rd-largest LatAm market, with about 60k connected vehicles. [S, company] — [Gurtam](https://gurtam.com/news/gurtam-top-fleet-management-provider-latin-america)
- Sascar / Michelin Connected Fleet: Michelin bought it for R$1.35B (USD 602M) in 2014. In 2022 it celebrated "300 mil soluções conectadas". [S] — [S&P AutoTech](https://autotechinsight.spglobal.com/news/26392/michelin-to-buy-brazil-based-telematics-company-sascar); [Mecânica Online 2022](https://mecanicaonline.com.br/2022/11/sascar-celebra-300-mil-solucoes-conectadas-na-fenatran/)
- Cobli: about 100k vehicles across about 5k corporate clients (date unclear), with a target of 500k by 2026. It invested R$20M in AI cameras and raised a R$175M Series B led by SoftBank (2021). [S] — [Mobile Time](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/01/11/2023/cobli-videotelemetria-com-ia-identifica-situacoes-de-risco-no-transito/); [Convergência Digital](https://convergenciadigital.com.br/mercado/cobli-aposta-em-videotelemetria-e-investe-r-20-milhoes-em-cameras-com-inteligencia-artificial/); [Forbes Brasil](https://forbes.com.br/forbes-tech/2021/07/startup-de-gestao-de-frotas-capta-r-175-milhoes-em-serie-b/)
- Brazil fleet-management market, paid-report teasers [S, weak, scope varies]:
  - MarketsandMarkets: USD 750.2M (2023) → 1,462.7M (2028), 14.3% CAGR — [MnM](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/geography/fleet-management-systems-market/Brazil)
  - Data Bridge: USD 1.02B (2024) → 2.84B (2032), 15.8% — [DBMR](https://www.databridgemarketresearch.com/nucleus/brazil-fleet-management-market)
  - Ken Research: USD 1.12B (2025) → 2.40B (2031), 13.5% — [Ken](https://www.kenresearch.com/brazil-fleet-management-solutions-market)
  - Mobility Foresights: USD 1.2B (2026) → 2.3B (2032), 11.5% — [Mobility Foresights](https://mobilityforesights.com/product/brazil-fleet-management-market/)
- Gerenciamento de risco: NTC counted 8,750 cargo thefts in 2025 (down about 17% y/y), with losses still in the billions of reais. The NSTech group (BRK, Buonny, Opentech) says it managed more than R$1.9 trillion in cargo in 2023. FreightWatch is cited saying security costs equal 12% of logistics companies' gross revenue (this claim is old, unverified, and probably inflated). [A/S] — [CNN Brasil](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/infra/roubo-de-cargas-cai-quase-17-em-2025-mas-prejuizo-segue-bilionario/); [Mundo Logística](https://mundologistica.com.br/noticias/gerenciadoras-de-risco-recuperam-340-milhees-em-sinistros)
- Video telematics and DMS in Brazil: Michelin Connected Fleet Smart Cameras offer fatigue and distraction detection with real-time alerts. Geotab's on-camera AI issues a **voice alert** for fatigue or phone use. Neither publishes prices. [S] — [Michelin CF](https://connectedfleet.michelin.com/pt-br/solucoes/smart-cameras); [Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/carros/como-a-ia-monitora-motoristas-e-combate-roubos-de-carga-no-brasil/)

### Inferences
- In Brazil, tracking for cargo is a requirement set by insurers and risk managers rather than a choice. Penetration in ETC long-haul fleets is therefore probably high, and Parceiro de Estrada would be sold as an **add-on layer** on top of an existing tracker (via the CCO / API) rather than replacing hardware. Incumbents (Sascar, Omnilink, Onixsat, Autotrac, Cobli) already sell fatigue cameras and are both channels and competitors.
- If Brazil holds about 35–45% of Berg's LatAm base (an assumption, not sourced), that is about 3.5–4.5M active FM units, including light vehicles.

### Gaps
- No free Brazil-only installed base from Berg Insight.
- No verified installed-base figures for Omnilink, Onixsat, Autotrac, Creare or Positron.
- No public revenue figure for the gerenciadoras de risco segment.
- Mordor and IMARC Brazil figures were not found.

## Q3. Price anchors (R$ per vehicle per month)

### Takeaway
Basic tracking costs about **R$60–100 per vehicle per month** (published retail prices). Fatigue cameras and video telematics are sold only by quote. A voice add-on would plausibly price at **R$20–50 per vehicle per month** to sit below camera DMS.

### Cited Findings
- Vivo Frota Inteligente (telecom, SMB): Rastreamento **R$59.99/month**, Eficiência R$79.99, Performance R$99.99, installation not included. (Year: current page, 2026.) [S, published price] — [Vivo](https://vivo.com.br/para-empresas/produtos-e-servicos/digitais/iot/frota-inteligente/compare-os-planos)
- Tracker hardware costs R$50–300 depending on the telemetry level. [S, weak] — [iCarros](https://www.icarros.com.br/noticias/geral/como-funciona-o-sistema-de-rastreamento-de-caminhoes-/29543.html)
- Geotab, Michelin and Cobli camera products are quote-only. [S] — [Geotab planos](https://www.geotab.com/pt-br/op%C3%A7%C3%B5es-planos/)
- Wage benchmark: median CLT driver remuneration is R$3,120/month (2024). Under the NTC cost structure, drivers are 19.5% of TRC operating costs, diesel 43.2% and vehicles 29.1%. [P/A] — [ANTT infográfico](https://www.gov.br/antt/pt-br/assuntos/cargas/dadostrc/infograficoperfilmotoristasrais2024.pdf); [SETCESP/NTC](https://setcesp.org.br/?p=204643957)
- Freight rates are about 10.1–10.5% below cost (NTC, 2026), which squeezes carrier margins and their willingness to pay. [A] — [Agência iNFRA](https://agenciainfra.com/blog/defasagem-do-frete-rodoviario-chega-a-105-aponta-ntclogistica/)

### Inferences
- R$30/month is about 1% of a driver's monthly wage cost, so ROI stories (fewer accidents, lower insurance deductibles, retention) can justify it. The 10% freight-rate gap means CFOs will demand hard ROI.

### Gaps
- No published R$ price for DMS or fatigue cameras in Brazil. The common market range (about R$150–300/month including camera rental) is **unverified**, so validate it with vendor quotes.

## Q4. Road freight market size and growth

### Takeaway
Road carries about **63% of Brazil's freight TKU**. ANTT's MDF-e count shows **1.4B tonnes and 749.4B TKU on 80.4M trips in 2025**. TRC was the largest revenue activity in IBGE's services survey (13.1% of R$2.7T in 2022, about R$350B). Growth is low single digits.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 (MDF-e, interstate and intermunicipal): **80.4M trips, about 1.4B t, 749.4B TKU, 33.9–36.5B km**. [P] — [ANTT Anuário TRC 2025](https://www.gov.br/antt/pt-br/assuntos/cargas/dadostrc/anuario_trc_2025_antt.pdf)
- 2025: CIOT records show 20.65M trips by TACs and equiparados. [P] — same source
- Activity growth: the IBGE PMS volume index for TRC rose about 2% in 2025 vs 2024 (105.15 → 107.24). Diesel sales were 69.4M m³ in 2025, +2% y/y and +9.87% since 2022. [P] — same source
- Modal split (2023, ILOS): road 63%, rail 19%, waterway 15%, pipeline 4% of TKU. [A] — [BNB/ILOS](https://www.bnb.gov.br/revista/cse/article/download/3245/2257/11345)
- IBGE PAS 2022: TRC was the largest of 34 services activities at **13.1% of services net operating revenue (R$2.7T)**, about R$354B (my calculation). [P] — [IBGE PAS 2022 informativo](https://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/periodicos/150/pas_2022_v24_informativo.pdf)
- GDP of transporte, armazenagem e correio: R$395.67B in 2025 (+2.1%). [A] — [CNT Radar PIB 2025](https://cdn.cnt.org.br/diretorioVirtual/94747915-7102-4fb1-86ad-901618f95d61.pdf)

### Inferences
- The sector is huge (about R$350B+ revenue) but has thin margins and slow growth. Software spend is a tiny share, so the product needs safety, insurance and retention ROI rather than "market growth" as its thesis.

### Gaps
- PAS 2023 TRC revenue is in SIDRA table 2577 but was not retrieved. No NTC revenue figure for 2025.

## Q6. Proposed TAM / SAM / SOM (Brazil, annual recurring revenue, 2026 base)

### Takeaway
On reasonable assumptions, the product as a B2B per-truck add-on has a **TAM of about R$1.2–2.0B/yr**, a **SAM of about R$150–250M/yr** (long-haul ETC fleets with a CCO and existing telematics), and a **5-year SOM of about R$3–10M ARR**. Autônomos add volume but little revenue.

### Cited Findings (inputs)
- Trucks + truck-tractors: about 4.1M (Senatran, Dec/2024) — [IBGE/Senatran](https://anuario.ibge.gov.br/images/aeb/2024/s5/2_pdf/s5t2102.pdf)
- RNTRC vehicles: 2.85M, of which ETC 1.88M and TAC 0.93M (Dec/2025, including implementos) — [ANTT](https://www.gov.br/antt/pt-br/assuntos/cargas/dadostrc/anuario_trc_2025_antt.pdf)
- Price anchor: tracking R$60–100/vehicle/month — [Vivo](https://vivo.com.br/para-empresas/produtos-e-servicos/digitais/iot/frota-inteligente/compare-os-planos)
- Drivers: about 713k CLT (2024) + 769k TAC (2025) — [ANTT infográfico](https://www.gov.br/antt/pt-br/assuntos/cargas/dadostrc/infograficoperfilmotoristasrais2024.pdf); [ANTT Anuário](https://www.gov.br/antt/pt-br/assuntos/cargas/dadostrc/anuario_trc_2025_antt.pdf)

### Inferences (calculation; the assumptions are mine, not sourced)
- **TAM, every truck in Brazil:** 4.1M trucks × R$30–40/month × 12 ≈ **R$1.5–2.0B/yr**. An alternative per-driver cut (about 1.48M professional drivers × R$30 × 12) gives about R$0.53B/yr. Using the RNTRC fleet only (assume about 55% of 2.85M is tractive ≈ 1.57M) × R$35 × 12 gives about R$0.66B/yr. This range should be stated as **R$0.5–2.0B depending on the definition**.
- **SAM, long-haul ETC fleets with CCO and telematics:** about 1.88M ETC vehicles × about 55% tractive ≈ 1.0M tractive units. Assume 40–60% are in fleets of 10+ trucks with tracking or a CCO, about 400–600k trucks. At R$35/month that is **≈ R$170–250M/yr**. The autônomo segment (about 0.5M old TAC trucks) is treated as **freemium / B2B2C via gerenciadoras or seguradoras**, at most R$10–15/month with low conversion. That adds perhaps R$10–30M/yr, but it is an assumption.
- **SOM, 5 years:** 1.5–4% of SAM, i.e. **6k–24k trucks ≈ R$2.5–10M ARR**, most plausibly through 1–2 telematics or risk-manager channel partners. One 1,000-truck carrier ≈ R$420k ARR at R$35.
- **Headwinds to state alongside the numbers:** the sector is fragmented (73% of carriers are TACs; ETCs average about 3–4 tractive units); TAC trucks average 24.4 years old and TAC incomes are low, with high debt (34% in 2019); freight rates are 10% below cost; truck sales fell 8.6% in 2025; and incumbents already bundle fatigue cameras with voice alerts.

### Gaps
- Validate the tractive-vs-implemento split and the fleet-size distribution from RNTRC open data.
- Get real DMS and video telematics price quotes.
- Penetration of telematics among ETCs (no public number).
